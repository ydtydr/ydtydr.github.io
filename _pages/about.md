Welcome to my homepage!

I am a senior machine learning researcher on the Apple Foundation Models pre-training team. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Cornell University, advised by [Chris De Sa](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~cdesa/). I'm also grateful to work with [Vitaly Shmatikov](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~shmat/) on machine learning privacy. Prior to Cornell, I obtained bachelor degree in Mathematics (ZhiYuan Honors) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I am fourtunate to be advised by [John E. Hopcroft](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/jeh/) and [Huan Long](https://basics.sjtu.edu.cn/~longhuan/).

My work spans efficient model architectures, accurate scaling, and low-precision training. I am also interested in building eﬃcient machine learning systems for large-scale foundation models, my goal is to make foundation models more capable and eﬃcient.
